Privacy Policy

Fred99 Publisher · last updated 13 November 2026

Fred99 Publisher ("the app") is a private publishing tool operated by Fred99 Productions. It is used only by the owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts: the YouTube channels, Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts and TikTok accounts of How Geography Works and How Building Works. The app is not offered to the public.

Platforms the app uses

  • YouTube: the app uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos, subtitles and thumbnails to the owner's channels. By using the app you are agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Facebook and Instagram: the app uses the Meta Graph API to publish videos to the owner's Pages and Instagram professional accounts. See the Meta Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok: the app uses the TikTok Content Posting API to send the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok accounts. See the TikTok Privacy Policy.

What information the app accesses, uses and stores

The app accesses only what it needs to publish: the access tokens each platform issues when the owner logs in, the basic account information needed to confirm the right account (channel, Page or account name and ID), and the IDs, links and publishing status of the videos it uploads.

This information is stored only on the owner's own computer, in files readable only by the owner. It is used only to publish the owner's videos and is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or transferred anywhere else.

The app does not collect, store or process any information about other people, such as viewers, followers or commenters, and it uses no cookies or analytics. This website itself sets no cookies.

Fred99 Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time: for Google and YouTube on the Google security settings page, for Facebook and Instagram under Settings → Business integrations, and for TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. The app's stored tokens and records are deleted on request and whenever access is revoked.
